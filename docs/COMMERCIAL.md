# Lotwise commercial readiness

## Buyer
Primary buyer: QA Manager / Head of Quality at a specialty food-ingredient importer, manufacturer or distributor. Daily user: QA specialist. Operations, procurement, finance and IT/security may influence purchase.

## Qualification signals
- Recurring incoming lots and supplier CoAs.
- Approved ingredient specifications maintained in controlled records.
- Manual comparison work or fragmented spreadsheets.
- Measurable review volume and reviewer time.
- Named process owner and budget authority.
- Willingness to evaluate using authorized, appropriately protected sample records.

## Paid evaluation offer
Scope one ingredient family, one approved specification set and an agreed document sample. Deliver measured extraction coverage, comparison results, exception quality, reviewer corrections, review time and limitations. Agree scope, data handling, success criteria, fee and payment terms before processing customer documents.

Do not accept confidential records until the customer has approved data-processing terms and deployment security is suitable.

## Recurring model hypothesis
Subscription priced by monthly incoming-lot/document volume and required operational support. Set pricing only after buyer research establishes volume, existing costs, procurement expectations and delivery cost. Pricing and ROI are not validated.

## Distribution
- Search-focused educational pages for supplier CoA review and specification matching.
- Public synthetic-data demonstration.
- Specialist food-ingredient and QA directories and communities.
- A small number of qualified design-partner evaluations.
- Track qualified inquiries, evaluation conversion, paid conversion, active lots and retention.

## Commercial proof required
- Documented buyer interviews and workflow evidence.
- At least one properly authorized paid evaluation agreement.
- Verified payment before claiming revenue.
- Successful evaluation against agreed criteria.
- Evidence of repeat usage and credible subscription conversion.

## Minor-founder operating boundary
Contracts, payment-provider onboarding and confidential customer records require appropriate adult support and compliance with applicable law and platform rules.
