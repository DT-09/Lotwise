THE REVIEW LOOP

From supplier file
to documented decision.

A controlled sequence that keeps the source, the rule and the reviewer connected.

01 / INTAKE

Collect

Register the supplier, ingredient, incoming lot and its source documents.

02 / COMPARE

Verify

Extract supported observations and compare exact test mappings with an approved specification.

03 / INVESTIGATE

Resolve

Surface missing tests, unit conflicts, identity gaps and potential out-of-limit results.

04 / RECORD

Account

Capture a qualified reviewer’s disposition, rationale and supporting evidence.

AUTOMATION WITH BOUNDARIES

Show the work.
Keep the judgement human.

Lotwise retains document fingerprints and extracted source text. Numerical comparisons follow explicit specification rules. Unknown units, conflicting observations and uncertain identities remain visible review items.

Lotwise does not certify food safety, replace laboratory testing, establish regulatory compliance or release product.

A MEASURED FIRST ENGAGEMENT

Start narrow.
Prove the workflow.

Evaluate one ingredient family against an approved specification and an agreed sample of authorized documents. Measure extraction coverage, exception quality, reviewer corrections and time spent before discussing ongoing use.
